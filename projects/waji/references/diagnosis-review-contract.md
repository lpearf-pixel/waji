# Diagnosis Review Contract

## Review output

A review presents ranked Hypotheses, never one hidden winner. For each Hypothesis record:

- statement and suspected component;
- proposer and source type;
- supporting Observations;
- contradicting Observations;
- alternative explanations;
- confidence and basis;
- missing evidence;
- safest next verification action;
- urgency and consequence of delay;
- rule, case, feature, or model version.

## Human authority

A named qualified person authorizes inspection, pressure testing, disassembly, continued operation, shutdown, adjustment, replacement, and return to service. A phone, portable terminal, rule, similarity search, or model cannot execute a high-impact action.

## Disagreement and escalation

Preserve technician, reviewer, rule, case, and model outputs separately. Escalate when evidence conflicts, capture quality is poor, context is non-comparable, the machine is outside known cases, safety consequence is high, confidence is low, or an action is difficult to reverse.

## Closure

Close only with an Outcome or explicit unresolved disposition. Closure records reviewer identity, evidence, work order, confirmed or excluded component, parts/adjustments, post captures when feasible, remaining uncertainty, and conditions that reopen the case.

Case similarity proposes verification directions; it never transfers another machine's Outcome to the current event.
