# Waji Validation Gates

## Gate 0 — Phone protocol rehearsal

PASS requires one complete phone-based protocol rehearsal with safe procedure, replayable raw evidence, machine/event linkage, structured Hypothesis, Decision, Outcome or unresolved disposition, and a documented follow-up attempt.

## Gate 1 — Initial case closure

PASS requires five complete cases, three explicit Outcomes, at least 90% required-field completeness, preserved technician reasoning, and no use of fixed edge or complex AI as a substitute for closure.

## Gate 2 — Portable prototype

PASS requires guided capture, quality checks, offline save, export, notes, photos, pre/post linkage, self-test, modular replacement, and BOM inside **人民币 1,000–2,500 元** or explicit evidence-value justification. It remains independent of machine power and control.

## Gate 3 — Portable field pilot

PASS requires two users, ten tasks, at least 90% valid capture, offline operation, no unacceptable safety issue, and measurable improvement over free-form phone recording in metadata completeness, repeatability, or case usability.

## Gate 4 — Diagnostic-direction value

PASS requires documented help to technician inspection direction in five independent tasks, preserved counterexamples and misleading matches, acceptable workload, and no process that converts similarity into confirmed fault.

## Gate 5 — Fixed-edge need and economics

PASS requires a persistent high-value precursor, evidence that human arrival or periodic capture is insufficient, a defined component and operating context, and annual avoided loss greater than full fixed-monitoring lifecycle cost including hardware, installation, communication, maintenance, replacement, cloud, review, and false-direction workload.

Without Gate 5 PASS, do not procure fleet-wide fixed nodes.

## Gate 6 — Small fixed-edge pilot

PASS requires a representative small pilot with stable power, installation, communication, data quality, linkage to field cases and Outcomes, acceptable review workload, safety approval, and tested rollback/removal.

## Gate 7 — Commercial expansion

PASS requires quantifiable maintenance value, reproducible workflow, lifecycle benefit above full cost, acceptable misdirection risk, customer willingness to continue, and continued technician participation in Outcomes and counterexamples.

Every gate records owner, review date, evidence links, contrary evidence, result, authorized scope, prohibited scope, and rollback.
