# Sensor Installation Contract

## Microphone repeatability

Record a position photo, landmark, distance, direction, height, enclosure state, wind protection, microphone/interface IDs, gain mode, cable state, and operator. Use a simple spacer, marked cable, positioning jig, or photo overlay when it improves repeatability.

## Temporary contact or vibration placement

Use a safe rigid surface with a repeatable landmark. Do not mount on moving parts, hot exhaust surfaces, rotating guards, high-pressure hoses, loose covers, painted flakes, or locations that obstruct normal operation or service.

Record:

- component and exact landmark;
- position photo;
- sensor ID and calibration version;
- axis orientation;
- coupling type, adapter, magnet, adhesive, clamp, or hand probe;
- surface condition;
- cable routing;
- installation and removal times;
- installer;
- action, load, speed, temperature, and repeat index.

## Rejected-quality states

Reject or flag loose coupling, unknown orientation, moved position, saturated signal, channel drop, cable impact, sensor rocking, unsafe placement, unrepeatable hand pressure, missing photo, missing device identity, or environment dominated by another source.

## Interpretation rule

A position, orientation, coupling, device, gain, or enclosure change is an alternative Hypothesis for signal change. Compare machine condition only after accounting for installation and acquisition differences. Never label deterioration solely from non-comparable captures.
